Official source and policy

Verified 18 August 2026 against the WWHS Assessment Handbooks page and the linked Year 7 Assessment Booklet 2026. The Mandatory Technology schedule is on PDF page 16.

  • Students receive at least two weeks’ notice for each formal assessment task.
  • A formal notification must include the nature of the task, assessed outcomes, weighting, due date and marking criteria for each component.
  • Use the formal notification for the task requirements and Google Classroom for your class post.
Issue and due timing: all three notifications are issued at the start of the applicable term and are due at the end of Semester 1 or Semester 2. Follow the task route and your class teacher's scheduled lesson or Google Classroom post.
