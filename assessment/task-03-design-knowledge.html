Task summary

Complete a 45-minute individual task that checks your knowledge of safe food work, nutrition, food-solution planning and evaluation. You will also apply that knowledge to short Fantastic Food design scenarios.

Course outcomes being assessed

  • TE4-SDP-01 explains relationships between sustainability, design and production
  • TE4-PDP-01 describes the practices and processes of designers and producers
  • TE4-MSC-01 explains how materials, systems and components contribute to solutions
  • TE4-DIG-01 demonstrates technological literacy to safely interact in digital environments
  • TE4-DIG-02 uses data and digital systems to code, design and produce projects

Relevant Food Technologies content is assessed through the task scenarios.

Knowledge and skills assessed

  • personal and kitchen hygiene, hazards, PPE and safe food work;
  • the six essential nutrients, fibre, water and adolescent food needs;
  • the Australian Guide to Healthy Eating and balanced food choices;
  • recipe reading, accurate measuring, workflow and practical organisation;
  • healthier recipe modification and justification;
  • evaluating a food solution and communication tool for appeal, function, ease of use and suitable content.

Completion conditions

Complete the task independently during the scheduled class lesson. The question booklet and response space will be supplied. Hand all task materials to the class teacher at the end of 45 minutes.

Preparation and adjustments

Revise the five course modules, section checks and written responses. Module checks and activities are revision, not the formal task. Speak with your teacher at the start of the term if you need clarification or an approved adjustment.

Policy and source

School assessment procedures, including absence, illness or misadventure and malpractice requirements, are controlled by the current WWHS Assessment Handbook. This notification uses the 2026 Year 7 Mandatory Technology schedule and the authorised Fantastic Food course learning content.
