Healthy Lunchbox Project

Task summary

Plan and prepare a healthy lunchbox for an adolescent. Your practical work must show safe and hygienic food preparation, correct equipment and techniques, effective organisation, presentation and cooperation.

Course outcomes being assessed

  • TE4-DES-01 communicates and evaluates design ideas and solutions
  • TE4-PPM-01 applies processes in the planning, management and production of projects
  • TE4-SAF-01 selects and safely uses tools, materials, technologies and processes
  • TE4-MSC-01 explains how materials, systems and components contribute to solutions

The task also assesses relevant Food Technologies knowledge and practical application.

Task outline and required evidence

  1. Complete an approved food order, recipe and workflow plan for an adolescent lunchbox.
  2. Bring the approved planning evidence to the scheduled practical lesson.
  3. Prepare and present the lunchbox within the available lesson time.
  4. Demonstrate correct equipment use, food-preparation techniques, PPE, hygiene and safe work practices.
  5. Manage time, complete cleaning and cooperate with others throughout the practical.

Completion and submission

Complete the task during the scheduled practical lesson. Present the finished lunchbox and hand your planning evidence to your class teacher at the end of the lesson.

Support and adjustments

Use the course modules, recipes and teacher feedback while preparing. Speak with your teacher at the start of the term if you need clarification or an approved adjustment.
