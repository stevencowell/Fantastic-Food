Task summary

Create a website or PowerPoint communication tool that helps adolescents understand how food choices support health. Your folio must communicate a clear purpose, use accurate nutrition information, show planning and design decisions, and evaluate the finished communication tool.

Course outcomes being assessed

  • TE4-DES-01 communicates and evaluates design ideas and solutions
  • TE4-PPM-01 applies processes in the planning, management and production of projects
  • TE4-PDP-01 describes the practices and processes of designers and producers
  • TE4-SDP-01 explains relationships between sustainability, design and production
  • TE4-DIG-01 demonstrates technological literacy to safely interact in digital environments
  • TE4-DIG-02 uses data and digital systems to code, design and produce projects, where appropriate

The task also assesses relevant Food Technologies content about adolescent nutrition and food-solution design.

Task outline and required evidence

  1. Homepage or title slide: identify the purpose and create an appealing design for an adolescent audience.
  2. Time plan: show what you planned to complete and when.
  3. Adolescent health advice: explain the six essential nutrients, their body functions, food sources and relevant dietary guidance.
  4. Recipe investigation: include a favourite fast-food recipe, propose healthier modifications and justify how preparation or cooking methods help maintain nutrients.
  5. Muffin design research: include the selected muffin recipe and workplan.
  6. Evaluation: judge the finished communication tool for adolescent appeal, ease of use, function and suitable content.

Meaningful diagrams, videos, extra recipes or food facts may strengthen the communication tool when they are relevant and appropriately acknowledged.
